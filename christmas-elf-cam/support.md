# Christmas Elf Cam: AR Video — Support

Need help with Christmas Elf Cam? Email us at **[info@appstackstudio.com](mailto:info@appstackstudio.com)** and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

## Quick help

- **The elf won't appear:** Christmas Elf Cam needs an ARKit-compatible iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Point the camera at a well-lit floor or table and move the phone slowly until a surface is detected.
- **Camera not working:** Make sure camera access is allowed in **Settings › Christmas Elf Cam**.
- **Can't save to Photos:** Allow Photos access ("Add Photos Only" is enough) in **Settings › Christmas Elf Cam**.
- **No sound in videos:** That's expected. Recordings are silent by design, so you can add your own music or voice-over.
- **Restoring purchases:** Use **Restore Purchases** in the App's settings while signed in with the same Apple Account. For refunds, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
