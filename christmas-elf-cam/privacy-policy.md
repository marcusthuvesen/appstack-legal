## 2. Data processed only on your device

### Camera and AR
The App uses your camera to show your room in augmented reality (ARKit), detect surfaces such as a floor or table, and place the animated 3D elf. Camera frames and surface data are processed in real time **on your device only**. We do not receive, store, or analyse camera images, and the App does not use face recognition or biometric identification.

### Recorded videos
When you record a scene, the video is saved in the App's private video library **on your device**. Videos are recorded without sound; the App does not use your microphone. Videos are never uploaded to us or to our service providers.

### Saving to Photos and sharing
If you choose **Save to Photos**, the App asks for permission to add the video to your photo library. This permission only lets the App add videos; it does not let the App read your existing photos. If you choose **Share**, the iOS share sheet sends the video to the app or person you select. Anything you share is then handled by that recipient or service under its own terms and privacy policy.

### Settings and preferences
Choices such as your selected elf, outfit, scenes, and other preferences are stored locally on your device.

## 3. Data we collect

The App has no account, sign-in, name, or email field. The data below is linked to random, app-specific identifiers, not to your name or Apple Account.

### Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)
If the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the crash trace and technical logs, app version, device model, operating-system version, language/locale, a Crashlytics installation identifier, and the time of the crash. Crash reports do not contain your videos or camera images.

### Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)
Where you have given consent (see section 6), Google Analytics for Firebase collects information about how the App is used — for example, which screens and features are opened, which elf, outfit, scene, or effect is chosen, whether a recording is started or completed, whether a video is saved or shared (not the video itself), and purchase-related events such as viewing the offer screen. This is collected together with an app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, app version, language, session information, and an approximate region derived by Google from your IP address. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and the App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

### Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)
The App is free to download. If it offers optional in-app purchases, Apple processes the payment. We never receive your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and unlock content. RevenueCat processes an anonymous, randomly generated app-user identifier, the products purchased, purchase and expiry dates, transaction and receipt information, the App Store country, currency, locale, app version, and device and operating-system information. We do not send RevenueCat your videos or other content you create.

### Support requests
If you email us, we process your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send so we can respond.

## 6. Analytics consent and your choices

Where required by law, we ask for your consent before Firebase Analytics collects usage statistics, and Analytics stays off until you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the App's settings. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; it does not affect data collected before.

You can also:
- allow or deny camera and Photos access in **Settings › Christmas Elf Cam** on your iPhone (the AR studio needs camera access; Save to Photos needs add-only Photos access);
- stop sharing anonymous App Analytics with app developers via **Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements**;
- delete all locally stored App data, and reset the App's identifiers, by deleting the App.

## 8. How long we keep data

- **Videos, preferences, and other in-app content:** on your device only, until you delete them or delete the App (subject to your device's backup settings, such as iCloud Backup).
- **Crash reports (Crashlytics):** up to 90 days.
- **Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics):** user-level data is kept for no longer than 14 months; aggregated reports that no longer identify anyone may be kept longer.
- **Purchase records (RevenueCat):** as long as needed to provide and restore purchases, meet accounting and consumer-law obligations (in Sweden, accounting records are kept for seven years), prevent fraud, and resolve disputes.
- **Support correspondence:** normally up to 24 months after the request is resolved, unless needed longer for an ongoing issue or a legal claim.

## 9. Children

Christmas Elf Cam is a creative tool for parents and families to make make-believe videos. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App has no accounts, chat, or social features, and purchases are made through the parent's Apple Account, where Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls apply. Children may appear in videos you record, but those videos stay on your device unless you choose to share them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you and receive information about our processing;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests (for example, crash reporting);
- receive data you provided to us in a structured, machine-readable format (data portability); and
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, email [info@appstackstudio.com](mailto:info@appstackstudio.com). Because the App has no account, we may need an identifier to find your data — for example, the anonymous ID shown in the App's settings or support screen, or an Apple purchase receipt. We normally respond within one month. Deleting data held by us does not cancel or refund an App Store purchase; refunds are handled by Apple.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Sweden, this is the **Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY)**, [www.imy.se](https://www.imy.se). If you live elsewhere in the EU/EEA, you may also contact the supervisory authority where you live or work.
